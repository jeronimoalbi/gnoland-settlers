Settlers

This is the testnet page: it mints on a chain made for testing, not the real Gno.land. For the mainnet mint page, see jeronimoalbi.github.io/gnoland-settlers.

A gnome for the first people of Gno.land. Anyone who had at least 15 GNOT in the launch balances of gnoland-1 can mint one Settler, free of charge (you only pay the network fee). It cannot be transferred, it's soulbound, it stays with the account that minted it.

1. The list of accounts

The page downloads the list of accounts that are allowed to mint, and checks it against what the realm itself remembers. This takes a few seconds and needs nothing from you. The file itself is on GitHub, if you would like to look at it directly.

2. Your account

Type the account (address) you want to check. The page looks it up in the list and tells you whether it can still mint a Settler, already has one, or was not eligible.

3. Mint

This is where you actually mint. Click "Mint" to open the realm's page with your proof already filled in, and send the transaction from there with your own wallet. Your proof is shown below too, it is safe to share since it only works for the account it was made for; if you would rather use a terminal, "Mint with gnokey instead" has the exact command for it.
